# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - First version

- **A Raids & Events tab on the guild window**, under Blizzard's own tabs: the guild's upcoming events, the soonest first, and those of the last week. Each row shows when the event is, its type and organizer, how many go in each role (signed up or confirmed), and your answer.
- **Events with sign-ups by role**: tanks, healers and damage, each with places or no limit, a minimum level, and a short note with each sign-up. Answer Signed up or Tentative, or remove your sign-up, from the event's view, as in the game's calendar.
- **Your role, ready**: the first time, the one your class and specialization stand for (the talent tree with the most points of your active spec group; a Feral druid tanks); then the one you chose last with that specialization. Sign-ups made in the game's calendar take it too.
- **Where it takes place**: a raid or a dungeon names its instance, PvP world PvP or a battleground, picked from a list with the game's own names. The day is picked in a month's calendar, up to the last day the game's calendar takes, and the time from lists as in the game's calendar (its 12- or 24-hour clock, minutes five by five).
- **Confirmations**: the organizer and the event's moderators give sign-ups the game's statuses (confirmed, standby, out or tentative; the organizer's too, as in the game's calendar) and their role, from a name's menu (right-click), or the role by dragging the name to another column. Every member changes their own role that way. An event can confirm sign-ups automatically: the first to sign up in each role while it has places, the rest on standby.
- **Moderators**, as in the game's calendar: the organizer names them from a name's menu, among those in the event's list; a moderator may name or take back others too, never the organizer. They change the event, delete it and give statuses as far as the game's calendar lets them, and are kept in step with it both ways; their clients keep the event in step with the calendar too, also while the organizer is away. Their own buttons: Confirmed, Standby, Tentative, Out and Remove sign-up; taking their sign-up back ends it, as in the game.
- **Locked events**: the game's Lock event, in the form. Members then cannot answer the event, in the addon as in the game's calendar; the organizer still can.
- **The game's calendar, both ways**: each event is a guild sign-up event in the calendar, kept in step wherever a change is made, also with the calendar's window open. Create, Save and Delete event change the calendar with the same click. Changes made in the calendar's window (title, time, type, description, lock, statuses) come into the addon, an event deleted there is deleted, a guild event created there comes in as its creator's, and sign-ups made there count. Your answer and the organizer's confirmations reach the calendar by themselves.
- **Weekly events**: a weekly event comes with next week's at once, and the one after is made each time one starts, so there are always two to come; each goes to the calendar with its Create in calendar button, in its view and on its row, off while the game takes no new event or after it reported its limit.
- **Deleting an event**, as in the game's calendar: Delete event, once confirmed, deletes it for everyone, with no getting it back. The guild master and the ranks with the game's officer privileges may delete any event too, as the game's calendar lets them (and nothing else).
- **The calendar first**: Create, Save and Delete event change the game's calendar first, and the addon takes the change once the calendar has it; when the game refuses it, nothing changes and the addon says why. The addon has no limit of its own; when the calendar refuses one for its limit of events created, nothing comes into the addon, and Create and New event stay off with the game's message until one of yours leaves the calendar.
- **Reminders**: a raid warning, a chat line and a sound before the events you go to: 5, 15, 30 or 60 minutes before, or none, in the options.
- **The notification mark** on the tab and on the micro menu's guild button while an upcoming event waits for your answer, and on the rows of the new ones until pointed at.
- **For other addons**: a public API (`GuildRaidsEvents`) to read the events and to bring in sign-ups made outside the game, through characters the guild master chooses in the options.
